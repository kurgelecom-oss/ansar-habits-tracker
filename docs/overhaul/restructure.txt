 The Notion question
 Keep it. Shrink its job.
 Notion stays where you write.
 It stops being what the board reads.
 Right now the child's board phones Notion live, every load. That is why a renamed column can
 blank the screen, why a normal Saturday reads as “No week loaded”, and why there is a wait
 before anything is usable. Notion is a great place to write a week and a bad place to serve
 one. The fix is one-directional: you keep authoring exactly as you do now, a job copies it
 into the app's own database every night, and the board only ever reads the copy.

 The order
 Seven phases, data before pixels
 The rule behind the order: a screen can only show what the system records, so the record
 gets built first. Phases 0 to 2 change almost nothing you can see, and they are what make
 phases 3 to 6 safe.
 Make it safe to change
 nothing visible changes1 session
 Before anything is restructured, stop the bleeding and get a safety net under the build.
 Ships: find out why 65 imported lessons produced zero October exam drafts. Stop the August evidence report showing on the default Progress tab. Stop the weekend showing “No week loaded”. Lock the dependency versions so two deploys can't build differently. Make the app buildable without live secrets. Write down what the product is and what the design rules are, in two files, so the next six phases argue against something.
 Gate: screenshots of every screen captured as a before. 401 tests still pass. Opened live, not just built.
 Build the record
 invisible to Ansar1–2 sessions
 The keystone. Five hours of school stop being one checkbox — but the points stay exactly the same, so nothing he's earned moves.
 Ships: a per-subject completion record, one row per block per day, keyed to the Notion row it came from. The existing single tick keeps working and keeps paying 5 points; it just becomes the total of the six rather than a separate claim. The new path writes alongside the old one for a week so you can compare them before trusting it.
 Gate: point totals for a given week are byte-identical before and after. The scoring and gating files are not touched.
 Switch what the board reads
 faster, same look1 session
 Notion becomes write-only. The board reads the stored copy.
 Ships: today's lessons, the week title and the subject guides all served from the app's own database. The four-second wait before the board is usable goes away. A Notion outage stops being able to blank the screen. A weekend stops reading as a failure.
 Gate: the stored copy matches a live Notion read, row for row, for a full week before the old path is removed.
 Give school a home
 the big visible one2–3 sessions
 School gets its own address, the way football already has one.
 Ships: /school with Today, This Week and The Year. Today's blocks carry the real task text and each one ticks. The week grid and the term arc come from data that already exists. On the dashboard, the homeschool section shrinks to a summary that links in.
 Gate: every habit configured in Notion still appears somewhere. Nothing disappears to make the layout tidy.
 Make them peers
 the restructure2 sessions
 The dashboard becomes Today: two pillars at equal size, habits underneath as scaffolding.
 Ships: one navigation bar instead of two stacked ones — and the hidden one that links to your ecommerce dashboard goes. The Weekday/Saturday toggle either works or is removed. The phone overflow and the clipped clock are fixed. Real Madrid stays, as context rather than as the biggest thing on the page.
 Gate: before-and-after screenshots at phone, tablet and desktop. No horizontal scroll at any width.
 The rituals
 the payoff2 sessions
 Friday recall and the monthly exam become occasions instead of an audit behind a locked tab.
 Ships: the recall gets its own screen, its own framing, and a sentence that tells him admitting a gap is the right answer. The exam gets a start screen that explains what happens if time runs out or the iPad dies — the system already handles both, he's just never told. Gaps start accumulating across papers into the Mastery map. The Parent Desk pulls Nihal's four scattered PIN panels into one door.
 Gate: approved papers and submitted attempts are never rebuilt. Practice stays isolated from the real record.
 One record
 both academies1–2 sessions
 Progress stops being a habit-tick percentage and becomes the record of both academies.
 Ships: school and football side by side across weeks and terms — blocks done, proof filed, marks earned, load carried. The hardcoded Targets brochure either gets wired to this or goes. A dated, printable evidence export that a registration reviewer could actually read.
 Gate: every number on the screen traces to a stored row. Nothing is inferred from a schedule.
 The guardrails
 How to do this without breaking it
 Most of what is already built is good — the server-side rules, the assessment engine, the
 honesty about evidence. These are the rules that keep a restructure from costing you any of it.
 One layer per changeNever move data and redesign a screen in the same commit. If a week's points look wrong afterwards, you need to know which of the two did it.
 Add alongside, prove, then switchThe new path runs in parallel with the old until they agree for a full week. Only then delete the old one.
 The protected files stay protectedScoring, gating and the tick endpoint do not change during a design phase. Those rules are what make the board hard to cheat.
 Approved exams are finishedAn approved paper or a submitted attempt is never rebuilt, not even to fix a rollover. Add a correction instead.
 Never delete a dated row without a snapshotA date-keyed copy must exist and be verified before any Notion row is moved or re-dated. Losing coverage is the one unrecoverable mistake here.
 Green build is not proofEvery phase ends by opening the deployed site and clicking through it. A successful deploy has never once meant the feature works.
 Test the value, not the keySecret values arrive empty in deploy previews while the names still look present. Checking that a key exists reports success while every write fails.
 Same screenshots, before and afterCapture every screen at phone, tablet and desktop before a phase and again after. That is how you catch what you didn't mean to change.
 Half-done ships offIf a phase can't finish, it goes out behind a switch that is off — never half-on. A child's board is not the place to discover an unfinished idea.
